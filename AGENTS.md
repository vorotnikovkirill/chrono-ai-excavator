# Repository Instructions

1. Keep all repository content in English.
2. Remain strictly within the current milestone.
3. Optimize for a fast, clear, and visually strong demonstrator.
4. Before adding complexity, determine whether it materially improves the final video or is required for the current milestone.
5. Avoid premature implementation of CAD import, real track dynamics, hydraulic-system simulation, flexible bodies, structural deformation, optimization, autonomous path planning, advanced physical calibration, and unnecessary frameworks.
6. Every significant milestone must update the README, living technical report, project time/tool/cost ledger, relevant tests, and preserved Codex prompt.
7. Update a presentation only at visually meaningful milestones.
8. Do not commit or push unless explicitly requested.
9. Do not add assets without clear redistribution rights.
10. Do not combine human effort, AI execution time, and computation time into one misleading total.

Use original procedural geometry and project-created assets. Do not copy a specific commercial toy set, use LEGO branding, include proprietary geometry, or expose confidential information.
